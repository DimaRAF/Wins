# Stride

**An AI-powered adaptive time planning application that helps users build long-term consistency through personalized daily focus sessions and behavioral motivation.**

---

## 📖 Overview

My Journey is an AI-powered productivity application designed to help users stay committed to their long-term goals. Instead of focusing on task completion, the app promotes consistency by breaking goals into small daily time commitments and continuously adapting recommendations based on the user's progress, energy level, and behavioral patterns.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many people struggle to achieve long-term goals because:

- Motivation declines over time.
- Large goals become overwhelming.
- Traditional productivity apps focus on tasks rather than consistency.
- Users lack personalized guidance that adapts to their behavior.

---

## 💡 Solution

Wins helps users stay consistent by:

- Generating personalized daily focus sessions.
- Breaking long-term goals into weekly milestones.
- Adjusting daily focus time using AI based on consistency, energy level, and progress.
- Providing AI-powered weekly reflections and motivational feedback.
